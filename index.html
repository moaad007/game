How to play

  • Make words of 4+ letters from the petal letters.
  • Every word must include the center letter.
  • Letters may be reused, but only the 7 shown letters are allowed.
  • The yellow-bordered petal is your bonus letter — +5 each time it appears in a word.
  • Pangrams use all 7 letters at once: +7 extra.

Scoring

4–2 · 5–4 · 6–6 · 7–12 · 8+–12 (plus 3 per letter past 7)
Perfect game = 12 words + 1 pangram.
